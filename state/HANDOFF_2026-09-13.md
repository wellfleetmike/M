## Gotchas learned the hard way

- pkill -f with the Obsidian path matches your own shell; anchor it: pkill -f '^/opt/Obsidian/obsidian'.
- Launching GUI apps from the Bash tool kills the tool shell (exit 144); use run_in_background.
- Obsidian keeps its vault list in ~/.config/obsidian/obsidian.json; it will reopen a moved path. It creates .obsidian/ wherever it opens, which breaks hashes of nu.
- The Reading unit header regex needs re.M; a first tagging run matched nothing because of it. Logged.
- The relay_comms self test is stale after step 5 (calls a peek argument the core never had).
- The record vault must be record/, never nu/: Obsidian parses every .md under the vault, and nu holds 61,000 curator block files.
